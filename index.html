About Vannam Digital Studio

WE SEE
IT ALL.

Vannam Digital Studio was born in Chennai with one belief — every wedding deserves to be seen in full. Not just the rituals. The emotions. The scale. The quiet moments between the loud ones.

We combine documentary-style candid photography with licensed drone cinematography to give you something no single photographer can: the full story of your day, from every height.

"Your wedding has a shape that only reveals itself from 150 metres up. We'll show you what it looks like."

How It Works

THE PROCESS

Discovery Call

We learn your vision, venue, family size, and key moments. We also check drone airspace permissions for your location before committing.

Week 1 · Free Consultation
Venue Survey & Planning

We scout your venue, map drone flight paths, plan optimal light windows, and create a minute-by-minute shot plan tailored to your ceremony.

2–4 Weeks Before Wedding
Wedding Day Coverage

Two-person minimum team. Drone pilot + ground photographer. We arrive 60 minutes early, stay until the last dance, and miss absolutely nothing.

Your Big Day
Edit, Grade & Deliver

Premium colour grade, full aerial + ground edit. Private gallery delivered in 4–6 weeks. Heirloom album hand-designed and printed on request.

4–6 Weeks After Wedding
